# Russian and English reader source: chapter 31, third ten frames

This reviewed English translation covers **GELFAND-P001405–GELFAND-P001414**, ten further held frames of chapter 31. It continues Schubert polynomials, two-dimensional categorical structures, derived categories, chaotic dynamics, matroids and Hecke-algebra representations. The unchanged Russian diplomatic material is supplied separately with the same useful diagram supplements.

All **26 displayed formulas, 167 inline expressions and 18 original native diagrams** are retained. All **40 historical reading records** keep their original Russian fields and full English observations, candidates and notes. Six previously described drawings now have explicitly partial native recreations. Eight small original details accompany those drawings and two existing native diagrams. The original seven-point markings and the irregular Young-tableau outline remain intact. Written material is typed; source excerpts supplement it.

Original resolution and 3×, 5× and 10× views leave some labels, retraced connections and circle contacts unresolved. Familiar mathematical terminology does not supply missing glyphs. The determinant formula retains its written indices and absent sum bounds. A separately labelled conditional check explains a discrepancy under the interpretation that its sum runs from 1 to d; it does not alter the source formula.

Include the relevant language's `fragment-macros`, `flowing-fragment` and `reading-provenance-appendix` files in a LuaLaTeX document with the packages listed in the macros and suitable Unicode/Cyrillic fonts. Keep all native/pair files and the small `figures/` assets at their relative paths. Use a separate document for each language.
