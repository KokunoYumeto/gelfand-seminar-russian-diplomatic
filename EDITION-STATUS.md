# Editions in progress

The checked source/transcription audit now contains 31 of the 34 chapter volumes: 01–17, 20 and 22–34. Across four checkpoints, these volumes contain 1,737 A3 pages and account for 1,440 source frames, with 297 continuation pages. The original page and diplomatic LaTeX transcription appear beside each other to support checking against the manuscript. The combined audit PDF and the other three volumes remain in production.

After the audit volumes and full combined audit PDF are complete, work continues on separate Russian and English A4 readers. Each will have chapter volumes and a complete combined PDF, with no page-count cap. Two complete English source checkpoints now cover **20 source pages, P1349–P1368**, including all formulas and **74 full historical uncertainty records**: [chapter 29, final ten pages](english/reader-working/chapter29-batch01/README.md) and [chapter 30, first ten pages](english/reader-working/chapter30-batch01/README.md). Their translation sources have been reviewed; final A4 composition and rendered PDF review remain pending. The previously published 1,400-page Russian reader is an earlier working release; it still needs illustration work, layout refinement and source review.

Both readers retain the complete content and explicit uncertainties. Written text, formulas and diagram labels are typeset. Useful original diagram excerpts supplement faithful recreations where practical; diagrams with unresolved details retain the original evidence and typed explanations. Mathematical interpretation is distinguished from diplomatic transcription.

The audit release verifies layout and mechanical source/PDF bindings. It does not claim that every difficult manuscript reading is settled. Existing source gaps, cancelled fragments and unresolved notes remain explicit.
