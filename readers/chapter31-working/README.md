# Gelfand seminar: Russian and English A4 readers, chapter 31

Two separate flowing readers of all **40 held source frames**, GELFAND-P001385–GELFAND-P001424: **72 pages in Russian** and **70 pages in English**. Written prose, mathematics, cancellations and diagram labels are typeset in LaTeX. Each language includes the complete **158-record reading appendix**, with observations, competing readings and historical review notes.

Thirteen selected original diagram details accompany typed recreations. Eleven drawings formerly represented only by descriptions now have explicitly partial native diagrams; two existing drawings also have original comparisons. Source uncertainties about connections, directions, labels and topology remain marked. Manuscript images supplement the typed text and formulas.

All **142 physical pages** were personally checked from actual PDF renders. Reviews of unchanged pages were transferred only after exact image-byte comparison; uncertain labels and layouts received 300 DPI checks. All 40 frame destinations, 158 full reading-note destinations, embedded fonts and original-detail pixels were verified in each PDF.

This is a working chapter edition. It preserves unresolved readings and mathematical inconsistencies, and includes a separate conditional mathematical observation about formula (6) on GELFAND-P001412. Layout validation does not claim to resolve all manuscript ambiguities. The complete side-by-side audit is published separately; chapter volumes and the full separate Russian and English reader PDFs continue in preparation.
