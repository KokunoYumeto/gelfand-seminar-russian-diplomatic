# Critical companion to the notes of the I. M. Gelfand seminar

A commentary on the seminar notes, one chapter per academic year: every talk summarised; every mathematical item of
the notes stated precisely, with what became of it in the literature and its present status (dated); a problem index
across the years; and a bibliography. Results marked "proved here" or "computed here" were proved or computed for this
companion.

The chapters are AI-written by GPT-6 Astra (ChatGPT Pro mode), from the AI-transcribed Russian diplomatic text of this
repository and the MCCME scans. They have not been reviewed: every statement, attribution and status in them is the
model's own and unchecked. They are read on the website <https://kokunoyumeto.github.io/gelfand-seminar-russian-diplomatic/>,
which also marks the pages whose transcription has been revised since a chapter was written.

## Files

- `years/GY<year>/`: the seven data files of each chapter, in the format of `kit/CHAPTER_FORMAT.md`:
  `GY<year>_talks.json`, `_items.json`, `_problems.json`, `_overview.tex`, `_development.tex`, `_corrections.csv` and
  `_references.bib`. The tag names the first academic year of the chapter.
- `kit/`: the chapter format, the LaTeX macros (`gelfand_companion.sty`) and the scripts that build a chapter and the
  combined reader.
- `reader/`: the combined reader's LaTeX source and bibliography.

The chapters present are the folders in `years/`; the website's home page lists the academic years with and without
a chapter.
