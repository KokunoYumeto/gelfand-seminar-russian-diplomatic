## Independent mathematical review

The root agent's stochastic development was reviewed independently without modifying its file. The exact two-state and three-state constructions, including the nonreal-pair circulant triangle, are consistent with the stated hypotheses. For the five-point list consisting of two ones and three copies of minus two-thirds, the moment sums are
\[
s_k=2+3(-2/3)^k.
\]
All Johnson–Loewy–London inequalities follow by separating parity: for even k, apply the finite-sum power-mean inequality to five nonnegative powers; for odd k and even m, use s_k at most two and s_(km) at least two; for odd k and odd m at least three, use s_(km) at least ten-ninths. The case m=1 is equality and k=1 has zero left side. Nonnegative realization is impossible: the two Perron roots force at least two irreducible blocks, each block containing a negative eigenvalue must also contain a positive Perron root, and the nonnegative trace of a block containing one eigenvalue one permits at most one eigenvalue minus two-thirds. Two such blocks cannot accommodate three negative eigenvalues. The root's precise proof handles the reducible cases explicitly.

The tensor annotation was also independently checked. Its cross-Gram matrix is the transpose of the product of the entrywise conjugate of the first coefficient matrix and the second coefficient matrix. The Schatten norm inequality gives the sum bound. For a rank-deficient target list, the unused row supplies the remaining Frobenius norm; for full rank, the constrained positive diagonal weights continuously attain the required reciprocal weighted sum. The zero target and dimension-one exceptions are handled separately.
