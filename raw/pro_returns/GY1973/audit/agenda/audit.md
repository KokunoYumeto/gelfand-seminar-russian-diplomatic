## Classification decisions

The rank theorem determines the finite-presentation thresholds including the boundary ranks and small finite fields: polynomial n=3 fails, n≥4 succeeds; Laurent n=2 fails, n≥3 succeeds. It is used as an exact modern resolution, while its historical introduction supplies the earlier chronology. This does not date all special cases to2013.

Coherence of SL3(Z) and unconditional coherence of SL2(Z[1/p]) remain classC. A conditional result is not a resolution. The checked version3 of Brody–Fisher–Mj–van Limbeek, revised7November2025, replaces the broader version1 statement found on an author site. Its restricted sets of places are essential. The chapter makes no assertion that a finite search proves the absence of an unpublished result as of the cutoff.

GL1(Q) is coherent because every finitely generated subgroup is finitely generated abelian; the negative rational-matrix obstruction applies from rank2 onward. The checked thesis's displayed Baumslag–Solitar relation introduces a square under diagonal conjugation, so that formula is not repeated as a proof. The classification is marked indirect where the original1974 observation was not read.

Scott's result preceded the meeting. Applying it to covering spaces supplies the coherence quantifier. The imaginary-quadratic example requires an explicitly marked reconstruction of the coefficient ring as the ring of integers; purely imaginary elements do not form the needed unital ring. Coherence of free and nilpotent groups does not require the ambient group itself to be finitely generated.
