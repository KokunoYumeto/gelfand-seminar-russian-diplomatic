## Independent mathematical checks

The notebook's hyperplane proof was checked without relying on Quillen's
different line-cover proof. On the fixed-line subposet, the natural comparisons
W ≤ W+L ≥ L give a contraction. A hyperplane H not containing L satisfies
H+L=V, while every proper W<H satisfies dim(W+L)≤n−1. Intersections of distinct
such hyperplanes have dimension n−2 and lie in the fixed-line part. Hence the
quotient and suspension steps work for simplicial subcomplexes, including
infinite fields with the CW weak topology. The dimension-two base case consists
of projective lines as a discrete set. Over F_q it has q+1 points, so ordinary
H_0 has rank q+1 and reduced H_0 rank q. This independently detects the missing
tilde in the Steinberg-module formula. The same induction gives
r_n=q^(n−1)r_(n−1), r_2=q, hence r_n=q^(n(n−1)/2).

These are checks and explicit reconstructions of old mathematics, not Stage 2
solutions of an open problem. No new computational experiment was needed.
