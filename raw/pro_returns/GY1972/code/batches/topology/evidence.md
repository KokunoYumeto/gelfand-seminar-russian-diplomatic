## Independent checks of our annotations

`checks.py` and `checks_output.txt` are included; they use only the Python standard library. The computations are labelled oursComputed, and analytical proofs in items are labelled ours. An initial attempt with an unavailable symbolic-math package was replaced by exact integer/rational coefficient checks and elementary numerical quadrature; no missing dependency remains.

1. The pencil conjugation was rederived by differentiating exp(−aλx)w: the mixed first derivative cancels and the squared parameter becomes d²λ². The output verifies the coefficients directly.
2. The Kostyuchenko norm formula was rederived by differentiating an explicit exponential–trigonometric antiderivative. It was then checked in both signs of gamma, n=1,3,8, by original-variable and endpoint-scaled Simpson quadratures at 4000 and 8000 intervals. The finest relative errors were at most about9.2e−11. These finite calculations corroborate the exact identity; they are not its proof. The holomorphic convergence argument uses the resulting coefficient bounds on compact subsets. The signed-series argument was strengthened after checking that ordinary convergence already bounds each individual term; separate norm convergence of the two halves is unnecessary.
3. The signed-system biorthogonality obstruction was checked abstractly by density of the positive half and independently at gamma0 by v(−n)=−v(n).
4. The extended-interval gamma0 counterexample was checked by the exact reflected-sine identity for every integer n; numerical samples at four frequencies and three offsets give cancellation within4.4e−15.
5. The negative E6 matrix was checked by exact rational elimination: positive Cartan leading principal minors2,3,4,5,6,3. The source, not the computation, supplies the cubic's additional two-dimensional radical. This independently confirms inertia(0,2,6).
6. The reverse-Adams counterexample received independent mathematical review by the parent agent.
