## Chronology checks

Lomonosov's note was received5February1973, before the5March talk. Berezin's
symbols paper was received11February1972; his bounded-domain announcement was
received14December1972 and presented19December1972, before its1973 publication.
The full Quantization paper was received6September1973. For these translated
journals the volume year and later English copyright year are distinguished in
the bibliography rather than silently conflated.

The APS announcement was received15March1973, eleven days before Gelfand's
report. Its general statement must not be treated as a complete proof in every
dimension in1973. Manin's1973 paper has a receipt-date discrepancy: Math-Net
records23May1973, while the English printed end line reads23/JUNE/73. Neither
date is necessary for the classification; both follow the March–April seminar
circulation, and the discrepancy is disclosed in the bibliography.

## Independent verification

1. **Vasserstein counterexample:** `independent_flag_check.py` was written
   separately from the early batch's checker. It covers four finite fields,
   dimensions3–5 and both quotient conventions (24 cases). The proof was
   independently checked over every nonzero unital ring, and rank-two
   minimality was re-derived. Both checks agree with the corrective appendix.
2. **Lomonosov and Berezin:** the early-record reviewer independently checked
   the scale inequality, the distinction between a contradiction eigenspace
   and the final invariant subspace, the Fock derivative order and h factor,
   heat constants, weighted projector measure, overlap ratio, and disk sign.
   The review also corrected the closed-submanifold wording in Henkin's
   embedding statement. These changes are incorporated in the author script.
3. **Modular periods:** a second researcher independently derived the
   two-term measure's finite additivity, the primitive-character cancellation,
   the real/imaginary parity, and the prefactor i times the Gauss sum divided
   by2πΩ. See `../buchstaber/p_adic_independent_audit.md`. The argument uses
   cusp integrals, not an unjustified convergent Dirichlet series at s=1.
4. **Arithmetic:** `check_arithmetic.py` checks5814 floor-measure refinements,
   143 power sums, the normalized Bernoulli recurrence, three exact zeta
   examples, and108 specializations of the root coefficient identity. The
   separate early checker adds a fourth zeta level and checks65 power sums and
   65 mixed-polynomial triangular systems, including n=0. All checks pass.
   The proofs, not the finite samples, establish the general claims.
5. **Topology cross-check:** the assembling researcher independently verified
   the pullback proof for the stable tangent difference (1972-289.3) and the
   S4 real-K-theory counterexample to the reverse Adams formula. The former
   does not identify chosen spherical trivializations; the latter relies on
   the erroneous universal quantifier over k and survives inversion of k.
