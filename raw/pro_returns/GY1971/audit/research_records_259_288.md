## Main identification and chronology decisions

1. **Quillen was already announced.** The full primary-author ICM text, delivered in1970 and printed in1971, explicitly gives the finite-field cohomology algebra, plus construction, Brauer lifting and Adams-fibre theorem. These occurrences are E even though the fuller Annals paper appeared inNovember1972. The ICM theorem, not the Annals publication date alone, controls classification. Fixed-rank notation in the notebook is replaced by the stable construction; the bare fixed-subgroup identification is false and has an explicit circle counterexample.

2. **Fuchs’s condition is relative perfectness.** The displayed condition is [G,N]=N for N=[G,G], not the stronger condition [N,N]=N. Ye’s exact later Corollary4.3 verifies the existence criterion, giving B without asserting historical priority. The separate simplicity/uniqueness conclusion lacks a comparison map or universal property and remains F.

3. **Birman’s local criterion is identifiable.** The1968 primary paper, Definition4.2 and Theorem4.4, supplies the mutual-subordination condition. The scan’s equality of functional-calculus domains is retained as an explicit sufficient form. The localized difference is written as a bounded expression, avoiding an undefined subtraction on different domains. Gelfand’s trace-class classification question is a separate precise problem: Carey–Pincus1976 was identified, but the full two-operator criterion was not retrieved. Xia’s author slides verify only the narrower purely singular diagonalization theorem. No claim that equality of absolutely continuous parts alone suffices is made.

4. **Artin–Mazur’s two comparison statements differ.** Theorem12.9 gives comparison after profinite completion for a connected pointed finite-type complex scheme. Corollary12.10 adds geometric unibranchness and identifies the étale type itself with the completion. The pro-homotopy category, rather than an ordinary integral CW type, is essential.

5. **The categorical lectures are prior foundations.** SGA4 circulated in1963–1964; Hartshorne’s derived-category account comes from the1963–1964 Harvard seminar; Artin’s Leray statement is in the1962 notes. Current Stacks tags supply explicit checked hypotheses where older book metadata alone would be insufficient. Product versus direct-sum sheaf axioms, the derived-localization colimit, graded signs and bounded-below injective models are distinguished.

6. **Morava mixes old foundations with a developing programme.** Quillen’s1969 paper gives the universal formal-group interpretation and Mishchenko logarithm. Morava’s announcement communicated22November1972 is unpublished, not a journal article. Its Theorems1–2 verify geometric height strata and maximal-order-unit stabilizers. Johnson–Wilson’s1975 paper independently identifies Morava’s1971 Columbia notes as unpublished. Later formal-group geometry is not used to pretend that arbitrary geometric fibres equal integral K-theory. The infinite-coordinate scheme also cannot automatically inherit a finite-type homogeneous-space theorem.

7. **Dyson’s two errors have different status.** The missing leading q in the eta product is a mathematical error in the notebook. The residue-labelled five-variable tau formula agrees with the primary identity. The word simple before positive roots is wrong: A4 has ten positive roots but four simple roots. The exceptional exponent26 has a later documented dual-F4 explanation in Monastyrskii1980, so the corresponding definite problem is resolved B.

8. **Dobrushin is a1973 occurrence of an earlier method.** Nelson’s1971 Berkeley presentation and Cartier’s November1972 lecture precede the corrected February1973 date. The reconstruction states the actual Sobolev extension, supported-boundary fields and energy-domain integrability assumptions. Euclidean invariance and an unspecified Markov property alone are not claimed to establish all Wightman axioms. Gelfand’s two programmes and the phrase almost Glimm–Jaffe lack an equation, dimension, cutoff regime or precise target theorem; all three are F, not manufactured open problems.
