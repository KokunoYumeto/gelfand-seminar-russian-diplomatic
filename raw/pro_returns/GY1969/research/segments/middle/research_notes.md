### Quantum states and factor representations

Powers1967, read in full Russian1969 translation at the relevant theorem and examples, is the exact pre-seminar factor criterion. P319's existential y is a genuine vacuity error; the required condition is uniform over all tail-commutant y after choosing a sufficiently large local algebra. All product states are factors, including cases without a uniform lower bound on their site probabilities. Araki1969 was verified through its primary publisher abstract, which states existence and primarity of finite-range one-dimensional Gibbs limits. Those claims are not generalized to arbitrary interactions or dimensions.

P312's direct-sum sign conflicts with its tensor-basis/partial-trace formulas. More seriously, commuting embedded finite marginals do not imply a simultaneous product eigenbasis when site spectra are degenerate. Root's Bell-block counterexample has been independently audited: every finite marginal is strictly positive, arbitrary disconnected subsets are covered, per-block embedded factors commute, and the simple top eigenspace is entangled. The valid simple-spectrum repair follows from polynomial rank-one spectral projections. Item1970-197.2 points to `dev:bell`; it is an annotation of a false literal E claim, not a manufactured open problem.
