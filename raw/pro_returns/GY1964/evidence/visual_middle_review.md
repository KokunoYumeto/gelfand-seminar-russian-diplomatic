## Result

**No visual defect requiring correction was found in the reviewed range.** No document content or layout files were edited.

- **Clipping and margins:** body text, formulas, item labels, class badges, and page numbers remain within the page and text margins. No truncated right edge or bottom line was observed.
- **Overlaps:** no overlap of body text with mathematical expressions, headings, class badges, or running page numbers was observed. The right-aligned class badges remain separated from long item titles.
- **Headings and page breaks:** long section and item headings wrap cleanly. Section headings remain with their metadata and following text. The four-line record-50 heading on page 96 is readable and has adequate spacing before the summary and first item. Ordinary continuations of item fields across page boundaries are present, but no isolated heading was found.
- **Mathematical rendering:** the Poincaré block matrix (52), conormal quotients (53), principal-symbol notation (56), KO/projective-space formulas (61), spectral and cocycle notation (65, 69), ensemble integrals and exponential formulas (77–78), even sine kernel and derivative primes (82), Jost/resolvent expressions (87), distribution and commutator notation (89), Cauchy–Weil kernel and wedge products (91), and sheaf exact sequence (94) display without missing-glyph boxes or collisions in the inspected PNGs.
- **Accents and typographic hierarchy:** accented mathematical names, Čech, Greek letters, blackboard-bold symbols, superscripts, and subscripts are visible in the sampled full pages. Bold headings, italic field labels, body text, and boxed class indicators remain distinct.
- **Whitespace and density:** the pages are consistently dense scholarly text. The larger gaps around section changes on pages 53, 61, 78, and 92 are orderly; they do not indicate an omitted block or a broken layout.
