## Reconstruction and checks

The exterior-algebra identities and scalar quotient are spelled out
from the notebook. The precise regularity and grading of an inverse BGG
algorithm are editorial reconstruction and are labelled accordingly.
An elementary n=0 example shows that the raw unshifted kernel in the
sketch is not already proper. This diagnoses missing grading data, not
an unsolved theorem or a counterexample to the published correspondence.

The contraction proof was re-derived using a complementary subspace;
the twist operation was checked independently in chain and cochain
indexing. Exact computation constructs 18 exterior complexes and checks
ranks and contracting homotopies. The same program independently checks
the SL(2) kernel and the fourth-order Lax commutator found by the other
record reviews. Its full structured output is retained.

No item in this segment meets the C/D development gate. The general
