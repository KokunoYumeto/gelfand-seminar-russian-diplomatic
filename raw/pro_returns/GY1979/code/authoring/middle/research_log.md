## Search method and principal identification decisions

The search record below preserves the query families and, where retained, exact query strings. It is a research evidence summary, not a claim to reproduce every intermediate search query. Searches were followed by opening original papers, author-hosted texts, publishers, or current institutional problem/review pages. A search-result snippet alone does not become a `read the result` verification. Formula locators were taken from retrieved full text or images, not inferred from article titles.

1. **Regular constraints and Hamiltonian quantization:** searches combined Dirac's generalized Hamiltonian dynamics, first-class abelianization, Gogilidze/Khvedelidze/Pervushin, Senjanović, Fradkin–Fradkina, Batalin–Vilkovisky, and BFV gauge independence. Dirac's original paper and the abelianization preprint were read. Generator replacement, regularity and locality are indispensable in 471.2; a passive coordinate change cannot make a given nonconstant scalar coefficient constant. The source question therefore receives a bounded affirmative local reading, not an unrestricted global conjecture.
2. **Gauge fixing and supergravity:** searches used Kallosh modified Feynman rules, Nielsen gauge identity, Faddeev–Popov gravitational ghosts, BRS renormalization, and Gribov/global gauge sections. The contemporaneous BFV publication leads (1977/1978) are distinguished from later Lagrangian BV antibrackets and antifields. The 1997 Bering primary paper explicitly assumes path-integral existence and excludes global Gribov problems and phase-space boundaries. Its hypotheses are retained when used to clarify the notebook. The 1975 BRS report has only three numbered main sections; unitarity/gauge-independence locators were checked in section 3, not fabricated sections 5–6. Original inaccessible articles remain indirect or unverified leads.
3. **Gravity, boundary terms, and Yau:** searches combined Gibbons–Hawking action/boundary, Yau 1977 Calabi, and Yau 1978 Ricci curvature. The Gibbons–Hawking scan and the 1977 PNAS announcement page were read visually. The unrestricted notebook Yau assertion is not rescued by silently changing its hypothesis; the actual compact Kähler/Ricci-form statement is supplied separately. The boundary correction requires fixed induced boundary metric and the selected action/orientation convention.
4. **Dilogarithms and regulators:** searches combined Beilinson regulators 1980, Bloch Irvine 1978, tame symbol, Matsumoto K2, ideal tetrahedron, Rogers five term, Apéry, Gabrielov–Gelfand–Losik, Bol webs, and Wigner continuous cohomology. Exact late checks included `Bol web dilogarithm five term functional equation primary`, `dilogarithm scattering theory 1979 Gelfand`, `Wigner Bloch dilogarithm continuous cohomology SL2 1978`, `site:arxiv.org "Bol" "Abel" "dilogarithm" Pirio`, `"Bloch" "Higher regulators" "continuous cohomology" pdf`, and `"Bol" "1936" "dilogarithm"`. Beilinson's 1980 section 1 equation (1) and Lemma 1 give a close publication match to 476.11, class A. The source's curve-zeta phrase lacks arithmetic hypotheses, a special value, determinant, and rational-factor convention; it is not promoted to Beilinson's full later conjectures. Wigner's cocycle was identified in Goncharov's author-hosted chapter, with its printed equation checked visually. Bol's explicit exceptional five-web was checked in Pereira–Pirio, section 6.1.4. GGL's original 1975 theorem was checked with condition (A) and linearizability intact; Goncharov identifies its dilogarithmic ingredient. The unspecified Apéry and scattering links remain unidentified mathematical assertions.
5. **Electroweak model and quarks:** searches used the original Weinberg 1967, Higgs 1964, Yang–Mills 1954, and Kobayashi–Maskawa 1973 papers, followed by the current 2026 PDG electroweak, neutrino, CKM and QCD author reviews. The original papers were read, including the weak representation assignments, scalar/vector mass relations and the unitary quark-mixing matrix. Modern neutrino masses do not validate a mass term absent from the minimal field content. Tree-level relations are labelled as such, and current measured masses are not substituted for the notebook's historical predictions.
6. **Flux tubes and confinement:** searches combined Wilson 1974 confinement, string width/logarithmic broadening, Gliozzi–Pepe–Wiese 2010, string breaking by light and strange quarks, and the official Yang–Mills problem. A fixed transverse width, a positive asymptotic static string tension in pure gauge theory, an effective-string expansion, and the existence-and-mass-gap problem are different statements. The 2+1-dimensional SU(2) numerical broadening evidence is not advertised as a 4d continuum theorem. Dynamical fundamental quarks permit string breaking; the reused problem is explicitly pure gauge.

## Access failures and verification limits

Direct publisher opening of the original Batalin–Vilkovisky, Fradkin–Fradkina, Kallosh, Senjanović, Nielsen, and Gribov papers returned access errors. Metadata/abstract leads and independently read later sources are explicitly distinguished. Their batch bibliography entries use `note = {unverified}` where the actual original publisher/source could not be opened successfully; root may independently improve metadata verification without converting it to proof verification. The Senjanović public reprint route returned HTTP 418; the Gribov academic mirror returned HTTP 503. A Singer/Project Euclid attempt produced an access-challenge HTML document, not a PDF; it is renamed `singer1978.failed.html` and is not cited or treated as read. No access restriction was bypassed.

Some public PDFs timed out in the browser but downloaded successfully from their public URLs: the Gogilidze abelianization text and the SLAC copy of the Nielsen-identity discussion were then read locally. The local `*.txt` extractions are supplementary evidence; where a sign or complex formula mattered, an actual page image was used. Publication dates, manuscript-received dates, and public preprint dates are not interchangeable. In particular, Beilinson's 21 December 1979 received date and GGL's 2 December 1974 received date are not asserted public-preprint dates.
