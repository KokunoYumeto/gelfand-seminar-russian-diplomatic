## Significant mathematical findings

The corrections affect more than typography:

- Matrix scalarization needs cyclic-coordinate or invertibility hypotheses.
- The auxiliary particle integral is a Bessel integral with a specified contour, not the recorded exact exponential.
- Rooting changes map-counting powers; the Tutte quotient has an index shift.
- A graph Gaussian needs zero-mode removal or explicit Dirichlet data.
- The exterior kernel has the tautological negative twist; the null-correlation example requires an alternating form.
- Repeated odd differential rows do not force a determinant to vanish.
- Not every Segre dual is a hypersurface; two-by-two-by-four is a counterexample.
- The local differential with the recorded plus sign fails to square to zero under ordinary left wedge multiplication.
- Kummer's quotient uses x mapped to minus x, not translations by the two-torsion group.
- Two transverse quadrics in P5 form a threefold, not the K3 example using three quadrics.
- Clebsch signs, exceptional grades, inverse domains and global topology must be handled together.
- Ising formulas require compatible signs, single-counted bonds, component factors, admissible surfaces and connected covariances.
