## Root mathematical checks and rejected identifications

1. **Digit arithmetic:** 3-adic truncations prove the expansions of minus one and one half. The 10-adic inverse limit is the product of the2-adic and5-adic integer rings; division by units remains possible. Exact modular tests are included.
2. **Spectral asymptotics:** the unnormalized off-diagonal kernel is not identically zero. The second binomial's upper index is n+k−2. A fixed smoothing order does not give an arbitrarily precise full expansion. The sphere jump rules out a little-o remainder but does not contradict the valid big-O estimate. The lower endpoint/self-adjoint/interior qualifications are retained.
3. **Hermitian forms:** the theorem is for complex Hermitian forms in finite dimension. Real2×2 forms give a checked counterexample to the unrestricted real reading. Simultaneous congruence diagonalization does not make both forms positive. The notebook's “angle” statement is qualified by strict separation from zero.
4. **Operator shell:** eigenvectors must be nonzero; an upper-height condition gives a norm inequality, not equality for an arbitrary bound. Generalized disks correspond to spherical caps, not one-dimensional arcs. Davis's relation-specific spectral-set definition is distinguished from the usual bounded-operator definition.
5. **Tori:** Wh vanishes while K1 retains sign and Laurent monomial units. A normal cobordism is not automatically an h-cobordism. Rational Pontryagin vanishing does not by itself prove stable normal triviality. The degree-one Hurewicz fibre is a finite torsor, not a subgroup. The Thom splitting needs a disjoint basepoint and binomial multiplicities in a stated stable range. “Infinitely many smooth structures on S2×S4” refers to a homotopy type, not one fixed topological manifold.
6. **Discrete series:** GL(2,R) is not semisimple. “i-dimensional cohomology” means degreei, not vector-space dimensioni. Sufficient regularity must address every root, not arbitrary signed simple-root pairings alone. The unspecified decay statement and “second Langlands conjecture” sentence do not identify an exact additional problem.

## Development and independent checks

The development rederives a **known** special case: the centralizer of q, automorphisms fixing q, the stabilizer of C(q), explicit inverse and conjugation formulas, and the condition for preserving A1. Dumas's exact stabilizer is §5.3.3, final Remark, printedp66; the formal-series construction is §5.2.2, pp59–60. The older locator §6.2.4/p70 was rejected after checking the full source. Alev–Dumas1998 is reused from the supplied cumulative bibliography. Dixmier's polynomial automorphism theorem is not promoted to a theorem about every division-ring automorphism.

The independent reviewer derived the full composition law, checked finite coefficient sums, generalized Vandermonde associativity for negative powers, the two-sided geometric inverse, and both inverse compositions. A second reviewer independently agreed. See proof_review.md and root_independent_review.md.

The exact symbolic run passed522 assertions. The first implementation expanded rational expressions before cancellation and became unnecessarily slow; it was stopped, replaced by exact rational cancellation, and rerun successfully. This was an implementation-efficiency repair, not a numerical tolerance change or alteration of the mathematical identities. A separate105-check editorial script verifies the Hermitian counterexample,24 adic truncations,15 Riesz coefficients and64 sphere multiplicities. The script outputs specify their finite scope; the universal statements rest on proofs.

## Search strategy and limits

Searches used exact titles, author/year/journal fingerprints and the statement's field/dimension/category. Math-Net translations recovered unavailable Soviet/AMS material; author-hosted PDFs resolved Davis, topology and homogeneous-space sources; original printed receipt and publication dates were read when available. Failed publisher requests were followed by legitimate primary mirrors or were honestly left metadata-only. A bare1954 Gelfand trace pointer was not converted into a plausible but unchecked citation.

Fresh current-status checks for the open questions were coordinated with the other fragments. The root additionally opened the2026 Lauritzen–Thomsen preprint and read Cor8.12, confirming index symmetry rather than an automorphism classification; the2026 Biró and2025 Chatzakos–Cherubini–Lester–Risager papers were checked against the pointwise circle question; and the2026 Damjanović ICM account was opened. The latter's relevant global-classification statement is in §1, Background, not §2. None of these checks establishes an exhaustive theorem about everything published by the audit date.
