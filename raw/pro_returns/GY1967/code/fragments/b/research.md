### Records143,150–154: sites, moduli and foliations

Mumford1965 is an exact historical source: §§1–4,6–7 closely follow the notes. The conference was Purdue1963. The author-hosted original is image-only, so selected pages were rendered and OCR-read locally. Site products and group-module cohomology were checked against the original, not a generic topic reference.

Corrections include the target of the base-changed cover on241; product instead of direct sum for infinitely many orbits on242; the finite-group/complex-coefficient acyclicity hypothesis in the H2-character formula; positive-degree qualifications for injective acyclicity and the local comparison criterion. The16September lecturer himself corrects analytic manifolds to analytic spaces, which can retain singular and nonreduced fibre products. Precomposition called f-star in the notebook is kept distinct from the modern geometric-morphism inverse-image convention.

The term polarized explained by a homology basis means marked. A point is also required in genus one to eliminate translations. Isom spaces supply moduli products, and a self-product counts every automorphism. Fine representability, coarse universality, versality and the stack/site interpretation are distinguished. The whole coarse j-line has no smooth pointed elliptic family with j equal to its parameter, because the local square/cube branching cannot lift a simple parameter. Isotrivial twists are a second obstruction to universality.

Integral analytic-stack cohomology on251 is H0=Z, H^(2r)=Z/12 for r>=1, H^odd=0; its ring is Z[c]/(12c), |c|=2. The entry explicitly verifies the periodic extension by Mayer–Vietoris for C4 amalgam_C2 C6. This is an elementary verification of a known computation, not new research. The retraced degree sign is left as a transcription ambiguity while the mathematical correction is fixed.

The assertion over any field concerns a different invariant. Mumford's Picard theorem assumed characteristic not2,3. Fulton–Olsson2010 Theorem1.1, preprint17April2007, gives Pic(M1,1,k)=Z/12 for **every field**, including2 and3. That exact extension is B; it does not extend ordinary integral Betti cohomology to arbitrary fields. A family pulls back the Hodge line bundle and a12-torsion class on its own base; it does not automatically yield a scalar modulo12.

Shafarevich252 is precisely reconstructed as a nonsingular holonomy-transversal groupoid/site, with its morphism convention explicitly qualified. Moerdijk's later classifying-topos theorem develops that construction, not the notebook's unqualified Euler formula for singular planar equations. In a genuine leaf fibration the transverse cohomology equals that of the base; the abbreviation triv. may describe this identification, not vanishing. The constant horizontal field on R2 has leaf space R and ordinary Euler characteristic1, while both cycle and singular-point counts are0. Thus the broad ordinary-cohomology interpretation fails. The intended support/reduced/relative conventions and singularity treatment remain F.

## Bounded development passed to root

Anchor **1967-145.3**, problem **GP-1965-08**. Known stabilizer, Dumas2011 §5.3.3 final Remark printedp66, citing Alev–Dumas1998:

q maps to theta(q)=(aq+b)/(cq+d), and p maps to theta'(q)^(-1)p+r(q), where ad−bc is nonzero and r is rational. These are exactly the automorphisms preserving C(q).

Proof mechanism: embed D1 into the formal pseudodifferential division ring C(q)((p^(-1);d/dq)), using Dumas§5.2.2 pp59–60. Expansions R=sum_(k<=m) a_k(q)p^k are unique. If [R,q]=0 and m is nonzero, its leading coefficient is m a_m(q) at orderm−1, impossible in characteristiczero. If m=0, subtract a0(q); any nonzero residual has negative leading order and yields the same contradiction. Hence the centralizer of q is C(q). An automorphism fixing q sends p to p+r(q). One preserving C(q) restricts to a fractional-linear automorphism theta; subtracting theta'(q)^(-1)p from its image of p gives an element centralizing theta(q), hence a rational function. The converse maps are invertible. Root is independently checking the further polynomial-subalgebra condition.

No assertion is made that every automorphism is conjugate into this subgroup. The full classification remains explicitly unresolved in the qualified status statement. The supplied previous index has no separately indexed proof of this reduction, but that is not evidence of novelty: the opened bibliography already gives the known formula. Any chapter proof should carry the required editorial proof marker and explicitly say it rederives a known result.
