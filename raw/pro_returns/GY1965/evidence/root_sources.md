## Searches and unresolved scope

Searches combined “automorphisms Weyl division ring/field,” “first Weyl skew field,” “Alev Dumas stabilizer,” and the Russian notebook terminology. Author-hosted expositions, the 1998 chapter, the 1984 paper and recent arXiv results were followed through their references. A recent discussion site was used only as a discovery lead, never as technical authority. No full classification was located in these searches. Therefore the November 2026 status is worded “no later full classification found in the verified literature,” rather than asserting an exhaustive impossibility result.

The 1964–65 occurrence `1964-38.1` concerns differing Weyl ranks. It is linked to the rank/parameter question. It is not relabelled as the fixed-rank Dixmier endomorphism conjecture. The supplied previous index contains no earlier GP identifiers, and the cumulative bibliography contains no reusable entries.

## Independent checks

The written development derives the centralizer, rational-subfield stabilizer, innerness criterion, quotient group and finite-group cocycle averaging with full hypotheses. The two runs of `code/check_weyl.py` use seeds 1965 and 20261109. They check exact rational differential-operator identities, an independently implemented action on monomials, positive and negative innerness examples, finite-group conjugation, and PBW counts. The two intentional negative controls detect both a wrong logarithmic-derivative sign and an omitted chain-rule factor. The separate written review is retained as `development_review.md`.
