Zumino–Wu–Zee's July 1983 preprint preceded the September report. Witten's August 1983 current-algebra papers preceded the relevant talks. ## Mathematical changes that affect interpretation

A congruence condition does not imply quasi-unipotence; the latter is a separate hypothesis. Full bosonic Fock space is reducible by parity. The Wess–Zumino ambiguity depends on allowed filling cycles: Witten's physical five-form normalization has some odd-pi periods on unrestricted cycles, while the allowed differences of fillings have 2pi-integral periods. A real skew-adjoint structure is essential to the mod-two kernel argument. A finite spin-fibre dimension 2^N does not count the infinite continuum spectrum. The Catalan count of pencils concerns a general curve, finite SU(2) groups require binary lifts, and a quotient singularity is a complex surface. The Poisson--Lie leaf statements have a closer match to the 1985 dressing-action paper than to Drinfeld's three-page 1983 note. A family-index map is not automatically a homotopy equivalence, and D*D loses chiral determinant phase information.
