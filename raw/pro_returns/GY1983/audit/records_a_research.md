## Main chronological and mathematical decisions

1. Deligne--Mostow's journal paper appeared in 1986, but the IHES archive dates its preprint to July 1982. The notebook lattice construction is therefore not classified as a newly open 1983 problem merely from the journal year.
2. The opened Faltings publisher record dates the 1983 article to October. Its relation to the 5 September degree-bound occurrence is partial: the exact notebook constant was not verified.
3. Vinberg's bounded-chamber dimension-30 theorem is explicitly in the 1981 original, Theorem 1. The 1985 survey's weaker displayed bound and references do not shift the theorem's date.
4. Witten's two 1983 current-algebra papers were published on 22 August 1983. Zumino--Wu--Zee's primary preprint is dated July 1983 and bears a September library stamp, despite the 1984 journal date.
5. Witten's physical five-form normalization was rechecked on p. 434. Its periods on allowed filling differences are not interchangeable with its periods on every closed integral cycle. Both occurrences in records 601 and 610 were corrected; the (-1)^N rotation phase uses the physical normalization.
6. Donaldson's 1983 paper does not prove uncountably many exotic R4 structures. The later uncountability statement is separated from the older topology remark; Taubes's full proof was not directly verified.
7. Cherednik's resolvent identity was read as Theorem 1.4 in the located 1978 original. The manuscript's "3.1" is recorded literally and treated as an unmatched reference number, since a different source could have a different numbering.
8. Krichever's 1980 theorem supplies a precise local factorization formulation for the nonlinear d'Alembert notice. It is not promoted to a global existence assertion across failed factorizations.
9. Hardy--Ramanujan's asymptotic formula and Rademacher's convergent series are distinguished. The notebook's unspecified multi-term formula remains only a partial identification.
10. The PCF density, bare Sato/MacPherson/Yau/Flaschka notices and unspecified soliton allusion retain their ambiguity. A nearby famous theorem or conjecture does not supply missing notebook content.
