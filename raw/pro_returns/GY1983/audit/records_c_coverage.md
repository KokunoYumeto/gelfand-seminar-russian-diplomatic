## Problem identifiers and development decisions

GP-1983-60 records the stated orthogonal-Grassmannian exercise; GP-1983-61 records the real mixed-Hodge Ext exercise. Both are solved and neither had an exact earlier problem occurrence in the supplied index. No identifier was allocated merely because a remark sounded suggestive.

Potential C/D candidates were examined rather than expanded by quota. The general deformation programme, polyhedra, volume, dilogarithms, localization, an even differential, almost irreducibility, further hidden symmetries, noninteger Rodrigues integrals, multivariable analogues, unbounded Toda, finiteness, infinite-dimensional geometry, a unification scale, functional integration and supermatrix Jordan form lack a sufficiently fixed statement in the notes. They remain F, with the missing data specified in each item.

The Casimir question has a conditional elementary answer for finite polynomials: the center of the derived affine enveloping algebra is C[c], by the PBW high-mode argument recorded in item629.5. Completed or fixed-level Casimirs require extra choices and are a different question. This elementary clarification is not presented as a new theorem, and the ambiguous historical question remains F.

Exact later matches classified A include Cherednik's elliptic representation construction, Gelfand–Zelevinsky models and hidden actions, the typical super Borel–Weil–Bott theorem, the identified twisted D-module continuation, Malikov–Feigin–Fuchs formal-power singular vectors, and the absolute-Hodge/Ext formalism. A bibliographic match is not silently upgraded to a proof read; the item fields and bibliography annotations distinguish the cases.

## Independent mathematical review

The Laurent-pole counterexample and three-dimensional Radon energy identity requested by the parent were checked independently. Full proof reviews are retained in the audit bundle. The first only refutes a bare sufficient-condition implication for compact abelian completion; the second is valid for measurable finite-volume sets with weak derivatives and almost-everywhere directions.

## Reproducibility and outage

The original local builder/checkpoint covered the early records, but the execution environment became unavailable before final files could be collected. All 107 authored objects were reconstructed in memory and preserved by exact readback from the private recovery Page. The rebuild script in this audit recreates the standard files from the core and audit JSON. It has not been executed in the unavailable filesystem. JavaScript structural checks listed in validation.json were executed; no TeX compilation, PDF generation or ZIP creation is claimed.
