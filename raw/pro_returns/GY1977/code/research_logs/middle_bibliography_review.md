## Verification flags

The chapter format defines `note={verified}` to mean that a reference was **opened**. It does not mean that the entire article or every quoted theorem was read. Under that definition, **no unsupported verified flag was found** in the current fragment entries.

There are **87 distinct keys marked verified and one marked unverified**. Every current key has a reference-audit entry. Across the combined audit, the strongest explicitly recorded check for each key is **60 “read the result,” 27 “publisher metadata checked,” and one “unresolved lead.”** These labels describe the evidence reported in the existing audit; this identity review does not independently upgrade them.

## Metadata observations

No blocking bibliographic metadata error emerged from this comparison. Several details that can look suspicious are deliberate and supported by their audit:

* `dayapremawaldron2026parabolic` uses `eid={12}` for **article 12**, not issue 12. Do not replace it with `number={12}`. If the bibliography style ignores `eid`, add a rendering-only article-number treatment rather than manufacturing an issue number.
* `salamstrathdee1975superfields` gives the verified start page 1521 and intentionally omits an unverified end page.
* `conway1976numbers` cites the original 1976 book while recording that the opened excerpt is from the 2001 second edition. Keep that edition distinction.
* BGG and some Russian-to-English references give a 1978 Russian publication year although the English scan bears a 1979 copyright. Their audits distinguish those dates.
* `narasimhan1962universal` uses the short publisher name Academia. A targeted check of the primary DML-CZ record confirmed the fuller form “Academia Publishing House of the Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences,” Prague, 1962, p.286. Thus no publisher correction is needed. Checked URL: https://dmlcz-proxy.ics.muni.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/700905.
* `mccme2026gelfand` is an access-year catalogue citation, not a claim that the historical notebook was published in 2026. Its audit scope is catalogue identity and dates.
