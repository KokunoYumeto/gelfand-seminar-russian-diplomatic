The genus-two search was repeated with the stronger search engine after the initially found Mironov and Davletshina–Mironov papers proved only family or generic statements. This exposed **Oganesyan 2016/2018, Theorem 3(2)**, which realizes every standard quintic genus-two curve. The primary full text was opened and the theorem read. Consequently occurrence 1980-519.2 and GP-1980-40 were changed from a provisional partial-development classification to **B**, with a resolved abstract-curve/standard-model status. The stronger arbitrary-affine-presentation reading remains false by the independently checked coordinate-unit obstruction; it was not converted into an artificial new open problem.

The universal curve-function-field question GP-1980-41 remains C in the documented search. Positive results include all curves of genus at most two and selected hyperelliptic families in every genus. A search hit about valuations on extensions of Weyl fields concerns a different question; a hit about differential-operator rings on curves gives embeddings in the other direction. Neither was treated as a universal solution.

The matrix-Hopf query found a 2015 paper on Riemann equations in bidifferential calculus, but the AIP page redirected to an inaccessible endpoint. Its abstract gives related solution-generating methods, not an exact general completeness theorem with the missing assumptions in Zakharov’s sentence. The occurrence remains F and has no invented problem ID.

Independent chronology review prompted a final Morrison check. The 1980 full paper was opened, and its p.385 Eq.(12) already contains the pure Vlasov functional bracket. The 1981 full correction explicitly retains that part while correcting the magnetic coupling and mass factors. Occurrence524.3 was therefore changed to E; the later geometric/gauge implementation remains distinct. Its statement specifies a rigorous cylindrical-polynomial functional core and does not claim PDE existence from the algebraic bracket.
