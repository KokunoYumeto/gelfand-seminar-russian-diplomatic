## Substantive findings and interpretation boundaries

1. The middle-perversity formulas onP917 andP922 are faithfully recorded mathematical errors, not silent transcription fixes. P922’s real codimension2i makes the missing minus one unambiguous.
2. Graev’s two FA references have different author lists. In real one-dimensional incidence fibers, closure alone is vacuous and the exact primary admissibility definition is the meaningful extra condition; no nonzero inversion period is inferred from closure.
3. Faran1980 is the exact Segre-family article, published before the13October discussion. Its detailed proof was not available; the adapted coframe correction is an elementary type check, explicitly separated from its publication match.
4. The20October regular Riemann–Hilbert exposition postdates Kashiwara’s22April1980 announcement. Finite-order versus infinite-order systems, derived constructibility versus simple intersection complexes, and irreducible versus reducible support are separated. The literal D-torsion-free criterion and unrestricted finite-length claim are false. The curve criterion’s quantifier is restored, not replaced by one generic curve.
5. The real-line projective countability statement is classical when quotienting by projective diffeomorphisms. It is false for equality of compatible atlases on one fixed line. The notebook’s detailed cover cocycle has missing conventions and is not certified by the corrected path-group description.
6. Gaveau’s precise sphere-filling theorem needs strict pseudoconvexity and the complex-point hypotheses. The literal torus prohibition fails for the product solid torus. The index-zero argument has no identified operator and staysF. The connection-space diffusion paper is identified, with its1979announcement/1980publication and the explicit1981author acknowledgment of these autumn seminars; no cutoff-free Yang–Mills construction is claimed.
7. The3November categorical D-affinity converse is answered in the10November continuation. The latter’s compactness condition is present, but its opposite-ring convention is wrong for left modules. Both errors are documented rather than hidden. Projective-space and flag-variety localization are repeated genuine occurrences.
8. The unextended GK conjecture reuses GP-1967-21. Premet’s necessary types A/C/G are not sufficient types. TypeA is positive; the checked2024v3/2025published source leaves C_n(n≥2) andG2 open. A separate2026Coulomb-branches result does not settle these cases. General GK is false.
9. Gindikin’s affirmative infinite-dimensional answer is consistent only when a unit is not imposed. The finite-dimensional four-algebra isomorphism list needs a unit, though the1/2/4/8dimension restriction does not. The explicit Hilbert tensor-coordinate example is exactly a published standard construction (Becerra–Moreno–Rodríguez2005Prop2.2), not a claimed new result or a reconstructed historical attribution.
