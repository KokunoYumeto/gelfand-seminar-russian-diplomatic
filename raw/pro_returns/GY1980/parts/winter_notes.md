## Important unresolved readings and evidence limits

1. **1980-502.2:** The dual-sphere equivariant derived-sheaf sentence specifies neither category nor kernel. The orthogonal dual spheres themselves are equivariantly identifiable. No Radon theorem is invented from this fragment.
2. **1980-505.1:** The four-operator assertion is false on its unrestricted literal reading. Primed operators and reducing subspaces are kept faithful. Lomonosov 1980 was checked only at metadata level; no exact theorem match was established. The Kato-based restricted repair and Weyl–von Neumann obstruction are explicitly separate from the notebook assertion.
3. **1980-508.2 and 1980-508.16:** Geodesic filling and quadratures lack definitions needed for a determinate theorem or conjecture. They are F, not newly created open problems.
4. **MacPherson original paper:** Gelfand–MacPherson 1982 metadata and abstract were checked, but the full publisher article was inaccessible. Actual statements in Atiyah and Zagier were read. Verification fields distinguish these scopes.
5. **1980-508.8–9:** The written dilogarithmic sign conflicts with unsigned volume on the given interval. An oriented convention might explain it, but no missing convention is claimed as recovered. The hypersimplex boundary formula is incomplete; full orientation signs are not silently supplied.
6. **1980-508.12 and 1980-508.15:** Real unoriented Grassmannian vanishing needs the parity condition in Atiyah. A Hamiltonian torus's own orbit has constant moment map, whereas convexity applies to the whole compact connected manifold or an appropriate complexified orbit closure. The source conflation is explicitly recorded.
7. **1980-503.4:** A Spencer-degree dimension symbol is ambiguous between source/ambient and relative dimensions. The actual complex and shift convention are specified without pretending a convention-dependent shift is a uniquely recoverable transcription.
8. **Newton formulas:** Genus, root count, and Euler characteristic require the stated dimension and nondegeneracy conditions. Mixed volume is normalized by V(Δ,…,Δ)=Vol(Δ), with root count n!V. Edge count, sign, and Hodge-index errors are flagged as faithful mathematical errors when appropriate.
9. **1980-513.2:** The inspected notebook scan has uppercase O. The 1981 English article's OCR can confuse O/o; regularity and the theorem were read, and no little-o improvement is asserted. A discrete one-dimensional count already rules out an o(1) error in general.
10. **1980-513.5:** Levi-curvature product normalization is suppressed in the notes, and S(Δ) can conflate the angular face factor with the body integral. A consistent contact normalization is specified as completion.
11. **1980-513.6:** At exceptional cancellation directions the indicator needs usual upper regularization. The fixed regular-system theorem of 1981 and averaged regular-space theorem of 2024 have distinct scopes. The 2024 publisher HTML has inconsistent displayed constants; it supports structural later development, not a replacement of the calibrated 1981 normalization.
