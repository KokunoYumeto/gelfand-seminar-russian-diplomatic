## Additional opened leads and excluded inferences

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10782 was opened as a 2026 coverage check. The revised Galois-rings/Coulomb-branches results do not establish the unextended GK conjecture for every symplectic enveloping algebra.
- https://www.ugr.es/~apalacio/pdf/on_urbaniks_axioms_for_absolute_144.pdf was read: it proves a different involution-axiom result and is not used as a substitute for the unital classification.
- https://www.math.u-szeged.hu/~stacho/HTM2/CIT/palacios94.pdf was opened, but the large scanned file did not provide reliable extractable theorem text. The filename is not evidence that the relevant long survey was published in1994; the author bibliography dates the survey to2004. Neither this misdated lead nor a conjectural bibliographic entry is used.
- The original Urbanik–Wright AMS landing/PDF and the original Gaveau–Trauber publisher PDF did not return usable full text. Their assertions were checked in accessible primary authors’ accounts with that limitation stated.
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021869397970538 returned403 in the final pass. It is the distinct1997 paper “Absolute-valued algebraic algebras are finite-dimensional”; it is not confused with the1999 conference review cited here.
- Mebkhout1984 “Une équivalence de catégories” full Numdam paper was opened during the Riemann–Hilbert search. Its infinite-order D-infinity setting is not silently substituted for the finite-order regular holonomic theorem.
- Original Kuiper1954 and the large ICM/Moser scan were not obtained in reliable readable form. The opened primary2025 authors’ proof and historical discussion support the real-line classification; the unverified1978 Faran thesis lead was removed from the occurrence data.
