# Winter primary-source research log

Audit date: 10 November 2026. Search families below are keyword summaries, except queries explicitly labelled verbatim. Opened metadata is distinguished from a read theorem. Bibliography keys are exact keys in `winter_references.bib`; existing cumulative keys were reused.

## D-modules and derived categories: records 500, 502, 503

Search families: Beilinson inverse/direct image D-modules; transfer bimodules; delta modules and point fibers; canonical-bundle right modules; Spencer resolutions; Kashiwara equivalence; finite-dimensional fibers and holonomicity; simple holonomic modules; S. I. Gelfand dual spheres, O(n)-equivariant sheaves, derived equivalence and Radon transforms.

- [Gourevitch lecture notes](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~dimagur/DmodI_2screen.pdf), with [Spring 2025 course page](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~dimagur/DmodI_5.html). `gourevitch2025dmodules`. Author PDF, 58 pages: Sections 7, 9, 13; Exercise 7.16; Proposition 16.6; Lemma 16.18; Theorem 17.2. Precise functors, left/right conversion, closed embeddings, fiber criterion, finite length and simple objects. A modern exposition does not establish the original discovery date.
- [Bernstein Chicago notes](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~bernstei/Unpublished_texts/unpublished_texts/Bernstein82-dmod-from-chicago.pdf). `bernstein1982dmodules`. Author-hosted 1982 notes, including Section 8, page 5 on Kashiwara's theorem. Lecture-note circulation is distinguished from journal publication.
- [Bernstein 1971 primary paper](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~bernstei/Publication_list/publication_texts/bernstein-mod-dif-FAN.pdf). `bernstein1971modules`, cumulative key. Thirteen pages, Sections 1 and 3: dimension/holonomic framework.
- [Weibel Chapter 10](https://math.mit.edu/~hrm/palestine/weibel/10-derived_category.pdf). `weibel1994homological`, cumulative key. Sections 10.1, 10.3, 10.5: complexes, quasi-isomorphisms, derived localization and functors. The concrete direct-map counterexample is supplied explicitly in the companion.

The dual-sphere searches did not identify an exact theorem matching 1980-502.2: the notebook does not specify the category or transform kernel. This is an identification limit, not an assertion that a definite conjecture is open.

## Analysis exercises: record 501

Search families: Thomae function powers and differentiability; continued fractions and Diophantine approximation; Baire first class and Dirichlet function; Fourier transforms of exp(−x^p), p=2,4,6, saddle points and steepest descent.

- [Varona 2009 full text](https://investigacion.unirioja.es/documentos/5bbc6b16b750603269e83309/f/6225f29d5fa07802f2a54cb8.pdf). `varona2009differentiability`, Gazette 36(5), 353–361. Classical Thomae proof, general-power differentiability, and reference to Nymann 1969 read. The exact denominator criterion in the companion is explicitly derived and is not falsely attributed to a particular historical formula.
- [Baire 1900](https://www.numdam.org/article/BSMF_1900__28__173_0.pdf). `baire1900nouvelle`, page 173 criterion read in the original article. Supports the obstruction to a single pointwise continuous approximation of the Dirichlet function.
- [DLMF Section 2.4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/2.4). `nist2026dlmf`, cumulative key. Section 2.4(iv), formulas 2.4.15–16, saddle-point method. Concrete phases and amplitudes are companion calculations with Fourier convention stated.
- [DLMF Section 25.12](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.12) was also opened while investigating dilogarithms; it is not used to certify historical MacPherson formulas.
