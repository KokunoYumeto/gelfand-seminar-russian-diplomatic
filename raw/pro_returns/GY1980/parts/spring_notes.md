## Important qualifications for integration

- Final independent chronology check: Morrison1980 p385 Eq12 already gives the pure Vlasov bracket. Item524.3 is E, with a rigorous cylindrical-polynomial functional core; the 1981 magnetic correction and the 1982 coadjoint/gauge construction are separately cited. The bibliography now has37 entries, of which Hilbert1901 alone is unverified.

- Oganesyan is the exact all-genus-two source. Davletshina–Mironov remains the stronger generic infinite-orbit result. Mironov gives selected hyperelliptic curves of arbitrary genus, not all curves.
- Makar-Limanov2021 Theorem1.1 concerns polynomial Weyl centralizers. It cannot alone certify the source’s unrestricted rational-coefficient coprime-order sentence.
- Grinevich’s rationality criterion allows constant gamma0; gamma0=0 does not force q=x. The exact potential sign and normalization in the notebook remain qualified.
- The Pauli weights with the tentative cn denominator were numerically checked once at regular real parameters (elliptic parameter0.3, crossing0.4, spectral0.2/0.6); the Yang–Baxter residual was2.22e−16. This is a consistency check, not a proof or a conclusive palaeographic reading, and no theorem is founded solely on it.
- The tensor-trace theorem uses the auxiliary legs and an invertible numeric intertwiner. The note’s trace3 is a faithful mathematical error.
- Bobylev’s exact linearization is for his analytic normal-coordinate class with isotropy and angular-cutoff assumptions. Countably many nonlinear invariants are not countably many ordinary linear collision invariants.
- Soulé’s bounds are presently valid, but the February1980 reference is unresolved. Weibel2005 Corollary95 and Table5.1 verify the order inequalities; Soulé’s1978 thesis only gives related restricted bounds.
- Lidskii’s1982 announcement is distinguished from a published proof; Knutson–Tao1999 and the Klyachko connection establish the final Horn result.
- Olshanskii1981 is a particularly strong exact match: properness and orientation of cones, covers, the contractive category, and the Cmin restriction in Corollary5.8 are retained.
- Hilbert1901 remains the only unverified bibliography entry because direct primary access failed. The occurrence has verification=indirect and only states the complete smooth isometric nonimmersion theorem. Root tried additional primary scans and agreed to preserve the explicit access limitation.
